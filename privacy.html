Privacy policy

Alfred is a private app used only by its developer. It reads the developer's own Gmail messages (read-only) to answer the developer's own questions.

It does not collect data from any other person, does not sell or share data, and does not send it to any third party for advertising. Data is processed on the developer's own home server. Access can be revoked at any time in the Google account's security settings.
